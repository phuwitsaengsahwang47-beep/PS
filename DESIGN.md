## Brand & Style
The design system reflects a precision-driven, contemporary print house and digital design studio. It bridges technical production fidelity with creative agility, tailored specifically for commercial clients, SMEs, and creators in Thailand seeking high-grade print solutions (signage, packaging, collateral, marketing prints, and brand identity).

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The visual language merges **Modern Corporate Precision** with a subtle **CMYK Process Nod**. Surfaces remain ultra-crisp, organized, and light-filled, while dynamic CMYK-inspired accents (process cyan, vibrant magenta, and amber warmth) infuse vitality without visual clutter. The interface conveys reliable machinery, color accuracy, transparent workflow, and approachable Thai customer service.

### Tone & Experience
- **Trustworthy & Systematic:** Clear status breakdowns, legible order tracking, transparent pricing tiers, and direct access to consultation.
- **Creative & Vibrant:** Tactile print mockups, bold typography accents, and rich chromatic badges for print substrate tags.
- **Accessible & Rapid:** Frictionless mobile-first paths into direct messaging (LINE OA consultation), fast quote calculators, and frictionless file upload interfaces.

## Colors
The palette takes root in the CMYK production paradigm, reinterpreted through crisp digital RGB space:

- **Primary (`#0284C7` - Process Sky/Cyan):** Serves as the bedrock brand tone. Used for core interactions, active state borders, primary submission controls, and brand trust markers.
- **Secondary (`#E11D48` - Process Rose/Magenta):** The energetic accent for promotional offers, urgent status badges, standout highlights, and creative print showcases.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Process Gold/Amber):** Used selectively for material finishes (e.g., gold foil stamp tags, specialty varnishes), review star ratings, turnaround warnings, and subtle decorative accents.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0F172A` - Deep Slate/Carbon):** Anchors typographic hierarchy, crisp high-contrast body copy, deep contrast buttons, and footer sections.
- **Neutral Light (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`):** Surface and canvas backdrops that mimic pristine, unprinted cardstock and calibrated proofs.
- **Success (`#10B981` / `#06C755` - LINE Emerald):** Reserved for confirmation flows, instant file verification, and the essential Thai LINE OA interaction button.

## Typography
Typographic styling uses `beVietnamPro`—an open, highly legible geometric sans with generous x-height and clear counterforms that harmonizes with modern Thai typefaces (Prompt, Sarabun, and Kanit). 

### Bilingual Harmony & Baseline Alignment
- **Thai & Latin Mixing:** Maintain equal baseline heights. Set `line-height` deliberately at 1.45 to 1.6 for body copy to prevent Thai tone marks (วรรณยุกต์) and sub-glyphs from colliding.
- **Numbers & Measurement Specs:** GSM numbers, dimensions (e.g., 9x5.4 cm, A3, 1200 DPI), and currency (฿ THB) utilize tabular figures or bold label styling to ensure quick scanning in print calculators.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements an 8pt architectural rhythm structured around a 12-column responsive grid:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, max container width of 1240px. Two-column splits for configurators (order choices on left, dynamic preview & price recap on right sticky column).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px edge margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px edge padding. Sticky bottoms for quotation subtotals and one-tap LINE consultation triggers.

Spacing scales proportionally: use compact steps (`space-xs` to `space-md`) inside functional components (order specs, paper weight chips, finish toggles) and wide breathing room (`space-2xl` to `space-4xl`) between presentation sections.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics the crisp tactile layerings of paper, vinyl stocks, and physical print proofs. Rather than blurry, muddy drop shadows, this design system uses sharp ambient illumination with low-contrast borders:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF` flat background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Containers):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp ghost border `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` and subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hover States & Interactive Panels):** Elevated via `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` alongside a subtle border shift toward primary cyan.
- **Surface Level 3 (Sticky Action Bars & Modals):** Crisp glass layer or solid card elevated with `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Floating Floating Action / LINE OA Trigger:** Elevated pill widget with subtle emerald directional glow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(6, 199, 85, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of `2` provides a modern, tactile, and engineered character.
- **Base Components (Inputs, Spec Selectors, Small Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px). Clean and structured, aligning with die-cut paper corners.
- **Cards, Estimator Panels & Upload Zones:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px). Friendly, clean, and distinct.
- **Modal Dialogs & Featured Portfolio Showcases:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px).
- **Badges, Filter Pills, and Floating Quick Contact:** Full round (`9999px`) for touch-inviting fluidity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0284C7`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600. On hover: `#0369A1` with an 8px transform transition. Mobile minimum height 48px.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #0F172A`, text `#0F172A`. On hover: surface tints to `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Accent Action:** Background `#E11D48`, text `#FFFFFF` for promo triggers, immediate sample box requests, or high-priority calls-to-action.
- **LINE Instant Chat:** Background `#06C755`, text `#FFFFFF`, fully rounded pill with the official LINE emblem and "ปรึกษาช่างพิมพ์ด่วน" label.

### Pricing Estimation Widget
- **Structure:** Modular card with segmented option blocks:
  - Material Selection (e.g., ไวนิล 360gsm, สติกเกอร์ PP ขาวด้าน, อาร์ตการ์ด 350gsm).
  - Coating Finish (ลามิเนตเงา, ลามิเนตด้าน, สปอต UV, เคลือบโฮโลแกรม).
  - Quantity Stepper / Custom Size Inputs with unit indicator (cm/m).
- **Price Summary Column:** Sticky badge highlighting real-time cost breakdown, per-piece unit price calculation, and estimated production turnaround (e.g., "ผลิตเสร็จใน 2-3 วันทำการ").

### Step-by-Step Order Process
- Horizontal timeline tracker on desktop, vertical stepper on mobile.
- Numbered circular nodes: Step 1 (เลือกสเปกงาน & แนบไฟล์), Step 2 (ตรวจปรู๊ฟดิจิทัล), Step 3 (ยืนยันพิมพ์ & ชำระเงิน), Step 4 (จัดส่งงานด่วนทั่วไทย).
- Active step marked with cyan badge and pulse ring; completed steps marked with emerald check icon.

### Portfolio Showcase Cards
- Aspect ratio 4:3 or 16:10 showcasing real-world photographs of finished work.
- Overlay chip indicates paper stock & finish details (e.g., "ฟอยล์ทองเค + เคลือบด้าน").
- Subtle elevation raise and 1.02x image scale on cursor hover.

### Inputs & Upload Dropzones
- **Form Inputs:** Border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, active outline `2px solid #0284C7`. Clear Thai helper text and error labels.
- **Artwork Upload Box:** Dashed border `2px dashed #94A3B8`, background `#F8FAFC`, displaying accepted formats: AI, PDF, PSD, TIFF with "ความละเอียด 300 DPI โหมด CMYK" verification warning.
